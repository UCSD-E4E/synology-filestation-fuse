Welcome to Synology NAS Mount

This installer will set up SynologyFuse, a filesystem driver that mounts a Synology FileStation NAS share as a local directory on your Mac.

The installer will place the following items on your system:

  • SynologyFuse.app in /Applications/
  • synology-filestation-fuse CLI in /usr/local/bin/

Administrator privileges are required to complete the installation.
